PUMP INSPECTION SOP

Document ID: SOP-PUMP-001

Purpose:
This procedure defines the basic inspection requirements for centrifugal pumps.

Inspection Requirements:

1. Check pump vibration during normal operating conditions.

2. Check for unusual noise or abnormal operating sounds.

3. Inspect the pump and connected piping for visible leakage.

4. Check bearing temperature and record the observed value.

5. Verify that lubrication is adequate.

6. Record all inspection findings in the maintenance report.

7. Any abnormal condition should be reported to the responsible maintenance engineer.

Escalation:

If abnormal vibration, excessive bearing temperature, significant leakage, or unusual noise is observed, the equipment should be flagged for further investigation.

The inspection report should contain the date, equipment identification, measured values, observations, and inspector remarks.
